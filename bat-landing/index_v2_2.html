Interstate Motorsport is an official Bring a Trailer Local Partner in Pennington, New Jersey. We can take your car on consignment and run the whole sale, or help with just one part of it: photos, video, preparation, an inspection or the listing itself. You choose what you need.

How can wehelp you sell?

Looking to sell your vehicle on Bring a Trailer? Choose one service or combine several. Select one to start your request.

  • 01 PhotographyA professional photo set for your listing: exterior, interior, engine bay and the details bidders zoom in on. Select →
  • 02 Photography & VideoEverything in Photography, plus video for the listing. Select →
  • 03 Vehicle PreparationDetailing and reconditioning by our Auto Spa and service team, so the car looks its best on camera and in person. Select →
  • 04 Comprehensive Inspection / Paint MeteringA full inspection with paint meter readings, documented so bidders can trust what they see. Select →
  • 05 Full Listing ServiceWe write, submit and manage your Bring a Trailer listing, and answer bidders while it runs. Select →
  • 06 ConsignmentLeave the car with us. We store it, prepare it, list it and handle the sale from start to finish. Select →

Full servicefrom keys to sold

Choose consignment or our Full Listing Service and this is what happens to your car, step by step. One team, one address.

  1. 01

    Bring it to Pennington

    Drive it to our showroom, or we pick it up with enclosed transport. It is stored in our climate-controlled facility from day one.

  2. 02

    Make it auction-ready

    Our service department inspects the car and our Auto Spa gives it a full detail, inside and out.

  3. 03

    Shoot every angle

    Professional photos inside and out, on location and in our showroom. The details serious buyers look for.

  4. 04

    Tell its story

    We write the listing, gather records and paperwork, and submit it to Bring a Trailer. You approve it before it goes live.

  5. 05

    Answer every question

    During the auction we follow every comment, answer bidders and add photos or video on request.

  6. 06

    Hand over the keys

    Once it sells we work with the winning bidder and arrange pickup or enclosed shipping, anywhere in the world.
